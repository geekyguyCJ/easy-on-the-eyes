Privacy policy

Word Search: Easy on the Eyes · Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your puzzles, progress, and settings are saved on your phone.
  • The free version shows a short ad now and then, between puzzles, from Google AdMob. Buying "Remove ads forever" stops all ads.
  • We don't sell your information, and we never will.

What the app keeps, and where

The app saves your found words, finished puzzles, the days you played, and your settings (text size, theme, sound, reminder time) in a small file on your phone. We never receive this information.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), that file is included, so your progress comes with you to a new phone. The backup belongs to your Google or Apple account and follows their privacy rules. We can't see it.

Ads (free version only)

Ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your internet address, never GPS), and which ads you see and tap. Google explains how it uses this at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and they always play silently.
  • Where privacy law requires it (for example in Europe and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Once you buy "Remove ads forever", the app stops showing and requesting ads.

Purchases

"Remove ads forever" is a one-time purchase through Google Play (or the App Store). RevenueCat keeps the purchase record so you can restore it on another phone. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt, but not your name or payment details. Google or Apple handles payment, and we never see your card. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash reports and usage counts

To fix problems and learn which puzzles people enjoy, the app uses Google Firebase:

  • Crashlytics sends a report if the app crashes: the phone model, Android version, and where in the app the problem happened.
  • Analytics counts things like puzzles started and finished, hints used, and whether an ad was shown. It uses a random app ID, not your name or email.

See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it locally. No server is involved, and you can turn it off any time in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • It doesn't need the internet to play; only ads, purchases, and the reports above use it.
  • It doesn't use GPS location, your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • It doesn't create an account or track you across other companies' apps for its own purposes.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved on your phone. For information held by the services above, email us and we will help, or use the links above.

Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
